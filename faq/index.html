Frequently Asked Questions

How does 100% Pay on Arrival work? ▾
You do not need to enter credit card details or send advance deposits online. When your 4x4 Land Cruiser picks you up from your hotel or residence, you settle the exact agreed rate directly in cash (AED, USD, EUR, GBP) or card.
What should we wear for the desert safari? ▾
Casual, loose-fitting cotton clothing is recommended (t-shirts, lightweight trousers, or shorts). Comfortable sandals or sneakers are ideal since sand gets into shoes. During winter evenings (November to March), carry a light cardigan or jacket.
Is dune bashing safe for pregnant women, infants, or seniors? ▾
By DTCM safety regulations, rigorous high dune bashing is not recommended for pregnant women. However, we provide direct gentle routes to the desert camp, or you can book a Private Land Cruiser where our marshal tailors the driving style strictly to your comfort.
Do I need an international driving license for dune buggies or quad bikes? ▾
No driving license is required. All Can-Am buggies, Polaris RZRs, and Yamaha quad bikes operate off-road in designated desert dunes with safety briefings, helmets, goggles, and lead guide escorts.
Are vegetarian, vegan, and Halal options available at the BBQ buffet? ▾
Yes, 100% of all food served is certified Halal. Our 5-star live buffet includes dedicated vegetarian and vegan selections, including fresh tabbouleh, hummus, fattoush, falafel, spiced lentil curries, grilled vegetable skewers, biryani rice, and fresh fruits.
