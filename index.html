Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers from our desert operations desk.

How much does the Evening Desert Safari cost and how do I pay? ▾
Our Evening Desert Safari is AED 120 per adult and AED 85 per child (ages 3–9). Infants under 3 are free. You do not need to provide any card or deposit online — 100% of payment is settled upon arrival directly with your driver in cash (AED, USD, EUR, GBP) or by card / Apple Pay.
What should we wear for the desert safari? ▾
Casual, loose-fitting cotton clothing is recommended (t-shirts, lightweight trousers, or shorts). Comfortable sandals or sneakers are ideal since sand gets into shoes. During winter evenings (November to March), desert temperatures drop noticeably after sunset, so carrying a light cardigan or jacket is advised.
Is dune bashing safe for pregnant women, infants, or seniors? ▾
By DTCM safety regulations, rigorous high dune bashing is not recommended for pregnant women or individuals with severe back/neck ailments. However, we provide direct gentle routes to the desert camp, or you can book a Private Land Cruiser where our marshal tailors the driving style strictly to your comfort.
Do I need an international driving license for dune buggies or quad bikes? ▾
No driving license is required. All Can-Am buggies, Polaris RZRs, and Yamaha quad bikes operate off-road in designated desert dunes. We provide comprehensive safety briefings, certified helmets, protective goggles, and lead guide escorts.
Are vegetarian, vegan, and Halal options available at the BBQ buffet? ▾
Yes, 100% of all food served is certified Halal. Our 5-star live buffet includes extensive vegetarian and vegan selections, including fresh tabbouleh, hummus, fattoush, falafel, spiced lentil curries, grilled vegetable skewers, biryani rice, and fresh fruits.
