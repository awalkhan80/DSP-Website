Frequently Asked Questions (Dubai Desert Safari FAQ)

Everything you need to know before booking your desert excursion.

How much does a desert safari cost in Dubai?

Standard evening desert safari rates start from AED 75 to AED 120 per person, including 4x4 dune bashing, camel riding, sandboarding, live entertainment shows, and a BBQ buffet dinner. VIP packages range from AED 150 to AED 250, while quad bike and buggy add-ons start at AED 150.

What is included in an evening desert safari?

An evening safari includes 4x4 Land Cruiser hotel pickup and drop-off, 30–45 minutes of red dune bashing at Lahbab Desert, sandboarding, sunset photo stops, camel riding, unlimited soft drinks/water, live Tanoura, fire show, belly dance, and an international BBQ buffet dinner.

Do I need a driving license for dune buggy or quad biking?

No driving license is required to drive dune buggies or quad bikes in Dubai. Riders receive full safety briefings, helmets, goggles, and guided convoy supervision. Drivers must be at least 16 years old to drive independently.

What is the cost of Abu Dhabi tour with Ferrari World?

An Abu Dhabi city tour with Ferrari World tickets starts from AED 280 to AED 399 per person. Packages include round-trip transfers from Dubai, visits to Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Abu Dhabi Corniche, and standard admission tickets to Ferrari World with access to Formula Rossa.
